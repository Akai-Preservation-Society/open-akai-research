# Code of conduct

This is a small community around rare instruments. People arrive with
different skills, languages and reasons for being here: hobbyists,
musicians, repair technicians, commercial developers, researchers.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with beginners.
- Argue about evidence, never about people.
- Give credit for other people's work, and ask before publishing something a person shared with you privately.
- Accept correction gracefully and offer it gently.
- Respect that some contributors sell products built on their research. They choose what to share.

## Not acceptable

- Insults, harassment, or discrimination of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Posting firmware, or pressing others to share material that [LEGAL.md](LEGAL.md) excludes.
- Passing off someone else's work as your own.

## If something goes wrong

Contact the maintainers through the organisation's profile page, or open
an issue if the matter is not private. Maintainers may edit or remove
contributions and, for repeated or serious problems, block an account.
